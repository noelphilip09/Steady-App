Sign in to the Host app on one device and the Couch Surfer app on another: accept a request as the host and it appears on the couch surfer's phone within a second, with a notification. The dashboard recalculates from the same records. Accounts you create through onboarding are real accounts on this server; the seeded demo accounts are listed on each sign-in page.
